⌁ Transponder Modes: Cockpit & Controller

Mode A · Mode C · Mode S · see what the pilot sets and what the controller sees

Controller view

Your aircraft is the one that flies where you steer it. With the transponder OFF or STBY the controller only gets a primary echo (+). Mode A adds your 4-digit code, Mode C adds pressure altitude, and Mode S adds your aircraft ID and 24-bit address. Emergency codes 7500, 7600 and 7700 flash on the controller's screen. IDENT makes your target stand out for 18 seconds.
