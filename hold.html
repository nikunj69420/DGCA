⟲ Holding Pattern Trainer

Random hold clearance · trail turns red when you leave the pattern · fictional VOR

Fly to the fix, work out the entry (direct, parallel or teardrop) and fly the racetrack with 1-minute legs. The trail is grey while you approach the fix, green while you are within tolerance of the ideal racetrack or an entry manoeuvre, and red when you drift off. Entry sectors use the 70° rule. Wind pushes you off the racetrack, so crab into it. Keys: ←/→ heading · Space pause.
